Accessibility

GPA Calculator aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page, signed in or out. Last checked on 17 November 2026.

What gets checked

  • Lighthouse Accessibility scores 100 on every public page and 95 or more on the dashboard.
  • axe-core reports no violations on the public pages.
  • Every action works from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring and a skip link to the main content.
  • Text and controls pass AA contrast in both the light and the dark theme.
  • Pages work at 360px wide and at 200% zoom without scrolling sideways.
  • Sign-in has no puzzle or CAPTCHA, and you can paste your password.
  • Before each release, the main flow is tested with the keyboard alone and with NVDA.

Known gaps

  • The NVDA pass for the current version hasn't been done yet. It runs before the next release.

Report a problem

If something is hard to use with your setup, open an issue on GitHub. Say which page, what you tried, and the browser and assistive technology you use.
